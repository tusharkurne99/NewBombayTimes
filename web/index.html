Today's Fastest: The Mini

Pick any name for the day, no account needed. Solve, and your time joins the board below. Land in the top five and things get a little celebratory.

Board resets each day at midnight IST.
New Bombay Times is an independent, non-commercial project built for fun. Not affiliated with The New York Times.

How to play

Pick a puzzle: Mini, Midi, or the full Crossword. Click any clue or grid square to select it, then type letters to fill it in. Arrow keys and Tab move between clues.

Pick a name for the day when you start solving. No account needed; it only has to be unique for today.

Your timer starts on your first keystroke. Use Check or Reveal (Square, Word, or Puzzle) if you get stuck. Solving without using Reveal puts your time on today's leaderboard.
